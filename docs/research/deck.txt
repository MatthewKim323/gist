THE PROBLEM
The system has all the information.
It's just not easy to digest.
Every note, email, task and document is captured. Case
management systems even give you a dashboard per case.
And still, "what's been happening on this case?" means
reading everything, or asking a colleague who was there.
SOLVED
Capturing the case. The notes, emails, dates, contacts
and documents are already in the system.
TODAY 'S PROBLEM
Digesting it. Turning a live case file into something a
human absorbs in ninety seconds. Nobody has solved
this well.

===== PAGE 8 =====
THE CHALLENGE
The goal of this challenge.
Build a solution that acts as a dashboard in order to:
01
Get the internal team members of a firm up to speed on a case.
02
Improve communication and visibility on the case for the medical providers
involved in the client's treatment.
Both halves. How you get there is yours.
This goes beyond an AI chat you ask questions of: the point is a visual digestion of everything already in the case, so the attorney
and the providers get up to speed without having to know what to ask.

RULES

when you submit.

THE THREE RULES THAT MAT TER
1
Everyone builds on one matter: Sapini. Your build reads it live from your Clio Manage account. Demo it in your video; it is also what
we evaluate on.
2
We read your repository. Features and code are verified from it. Hardcoded features are quickly identified.
3
Clio is only your input. Read everything, write nothing: API calls that read are fine, API calls that write or update case data are not.
Need a database? Bring your own, outside Clio.

HOW TO SUBMIT
A repo, a clip, and a few straight answers.
01
Your GitHub repository. The repo is the submission — we read it.
02
A 90-second clip: a quick rundown of your solution, on Sapini, and the visuals behind its

03
Your tech stack: built with, running on, and where data lives outside Clio.
04
Which AI models run your digestion, and the approximate cost to run one case. Rough
